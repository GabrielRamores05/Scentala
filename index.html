Our story

Born on 14 January 2026.

SCENTÁLA began as an academic requirement and became a real idea: how do you wear a scent without carrying a bottle, spilling it on your uniform, or filling a classroom with someone else's headache? Ten students of the Accountancy, Business and Management strand at Ateneo de Naga University — AC1A in SY 2025–2026, AC2A in SY 2026–2027 — answered it with a bracelet.

The trade name joins the English “scent” with the Filipino “tala” — star. Fragrance made wearable; a little light you carry with you. Every bead is made in-house, from beeswax, shea butter, jojoba oil and bamboo charcoal powder, coloured and scented to order, then sealed with a water-based gloss.

Mission

SCENTÁLA is dedicated to empowering students and young individuals through affordable scented bracelets that inspire confidence, calmness and self-expression — fusing creativity with responsibility for our customers and our community.

Vision

A student-driven fragrance-accessory brand recognised for turning identity into innovative, safe, mood-enhancing scented bracelets — leading sustainable, functional design that elevates Filipino creativity.

SustainabilityA solid alternative to liquid perfume — no sprays, no single-use packaging, made in-house with precise control over every material.
TransparencyWe share our bead-making research, our process and our sources, so you know exactly what you are wearing.
AccountabilityFrom the first mould to the final assembly, every piece is made under our own supervision and signed by our hands.
ConsistencyStrict batch control keeps every bead uniform in size and aromatic strength — no weak links.
DurabilityA high-density solid bead that resists cracking and holds its scent through daily wear.
InclusivityNo alcohol mists, no direct skin contact: fine fragrance reimagined for sensitive skin and sensitive lungs.

Rooted in Laudato Si' — care for our common home — and aligned with SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities).
